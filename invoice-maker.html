Privacy Policy

Invoice Maker for Android
Effective date: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how the Invoice Maker app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us").

In short

  • The app works fully offline and has no account or sign in.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Information you enter

To create invoices and estimates, you may enter the following into the app:

  • Your business details: name, email, phone number, address, tax number, website, logo and signature.
  • Your customers' details: name, email, phone number, address, tax number and notes.
  • Products and services you sell, with prices, discounts and tax rates.
  • Invoices and estimates, including amounts, dates, payment details, payment terms and notes.
  • Your app settings, such as currency, date format and theme.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server. We cannot see it, access it or recover it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or usage information. The app does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking tools, and it does not connect to the internet.

Google Play may collect information when you download or use apps, such as installation data and crash reports, under Google's Privacy Policy. This is handled by Google, not by the app.

3. Permissions

The app does not ask for any permissions. It uses standard Android features that do not need permission:

  • Photo picker: to choose a logo image. Only the image you choose is copied into the app. The app cannot see your other photos.
  • File picker: to open a backup file you choose.
  • Share sheet: to send a PDF or a backup file to another app you choose.
  • Print service: to print a document.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

4. When information leaves your device

Information only leaves your device when you choose to send it:

  • Sharing a document: when you share a PDF, it is sent to the app you pick, such as email or a messaging app. That app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Exporting a backup: the backup file contains all your app data. It is saved or sent wherever you choose. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Printing: the document is sent to the print service or printer you choose.
  • Outside links: the privacy policy link, feedback email and Play Store link open in your browser, email app or the Play Store.
  • Android device backup: if you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. This is controlled by you in your device settings and is handled by Google.

5. Data security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Because no data is sent to us, there is no server that could be breached. You are responsible for the security of your device and of any files you export or share.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete single invoices, estimates, customers or items in the app.
  • Delete all data in the app from Settings > Backup and restore > Delete all data.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all its data from your device.

The app has no account, so there is no account to delete. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data. Because all data is stored only on your device, you can use these rights directly in the app: view and edit your data, delete it, or export it as a backup file.

8. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app does not collect information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If the app ever starts to collect data, this policy will be updated before that change is released.
